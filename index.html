    How the score works

    • Quality: Google rating, pulled toward the 3.62 group average when a club has few reviews, in standard deviations from the group.
    • Walk: straight-line distance × 1.25 for the street grid, at 3 mph. Each 10 minutes costs half a point.
    • Crowd age: median age of residents in the club's census tract (ACS 2024). Each 10 years between that and your age costs a point. Clubs with a big after-8pm crowd (Google Popular Times) read slightly younger.

    Members aren't residents, ratings are noisy, and nine clubs is a small set. Use this to pick which two clubs to try, then visit both at the hour you'd actually go.
