      Quality

      The quality score starts from each club's Google rating. Clubs with only a few reviews are pulled toward the average rating of all Equinox clubs, so that a handful of glowing reviews does not put a new club at the top. The adjusted rating is then compared with every other club, and a club that rates well above the group gets a positive score.

      Walk

      The walk score estimates how long it takes to walk from your address to each club. It measures the straight-line distance, adds 25 percent to account for the street grid, and assumes a walking pace of three miles per hour. Each ten minutes of walking lowers a club's score by one point. Until you add an address, walking time is left out of the ranking.

      Crowd age

      The crowd age score compares your age with the median age of the people who live within about a quarter mile of each club, using the Census Bureau's 2024 American Community Survey. Each ten years of difference lowers a club's score by one point. The Census only covers the United States, so clubs in Canada and London are scored without this factor.

      This is an independent project and is not affiliated with Equinox. Club locations come from equinox.com and ratings from Google, both collected in November 2026. The people who live near a club are not necessarily its members, and Google ratings reflect billing and staff experiences as well as the gym itself. The best use of this ranking is to choose two clubs to try in person at the time of day you would normally work out.
